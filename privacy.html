Privacy policy

Effective 24 August 2026 · Last reviewed 26 November 2026 · Applies to BiblioHarvest 2.9.x for Windows and to this website

BiblioHarvest is a local-first desktop application. Searches, curated files, exported workbooks and API keys stay on the computer where the app is installed, unless you deliberately send information to an external service as described below. There is no BiblioHarvest account and no developer-operated server that stores your research data.

Who is responsible

BiblioHarvest is published by an individual developer ("the publisher", "we"). Contact details are at the end of this page. The publisher decides how the small amount of information described here is handled, and is the data controller for that purpose.

Information stored on your computer

The application keeps the following in %LOCALAPPDATA%\BiblioHarvest:

  • search results, curated sheets and exported workbooks you create;
  • your free daily allowance, credit balance and licence status;
  • free-tier counters: how many free uses each feature has had and how many sponsored unlocks were used today;
  • advertising frequency counters;
  • API keys you enter for an AI provider;
  • diagnostic log files.

These files are not transmitted to the publisher. They are ordinary files on your disk: anyone with access to your Windows account and that folder can read them, so treat a machine shared with others accordingly.

Network requests the app makes

The app contacts external services only to perform the action you asked for:

  • Literature databases — PubMed, Europe PMC, OpenAlex, Crossref and arXiv receive your search terms and return results.
  • Publisher and repository sites — when you request a PDF, the app contacts the site hosting it.
  • AI providers (optional) — if you enable Gemini, Groq, OpenAI or a local Ollama model, the text you choose to analyse is sent to that provider under your own account and key. Nothing is sent to an AI provider while the feature is unset.
  • Microsoft Store — the Store version asks the Store whether the signed-in Microsoft account owns the app or has purchased credits. The publisher does not receive the account identity or the payment details.
  • Publisher version check — at startup the app fetches one small file from this website to learn the minimum supported version. The request carries no account, identifier or research data, and if it fails the app starts normally.

Each of those services has its own privacy and retention terms, and acts as an independent controller of what it receives. Your IP address is visible to any service you contact, as it is with a web browser.

Advertising

The free tier shows one dismissible sponsored line, and an optional sponsored view that unlocks one metered action: a database search, an initial Excel export, or one use of AI, PDF download or analysis after the three free uses each of those includes. Watching is always optional — declining simply leaves that action undone, nothing is permanently locked, and the rest of the app keeps working.

The version distributed by the publisher displays the publisher's own promotional messages only. No advertising identifier, cookie or usage report leaves your computer for advertising, and no third-party advertising network is involved in the current build.

If a future version connects a third-party advertising network, that network may receive technical information such as your IP address, approximate location and advertising identifier, together with your interaction with the advertisement. In that case the provider will be named in this policy and in the Store listing, the effective date above will change before the update ships, and options to limit interest-based advertising — including the advertising-identifier and privacy settings in Windows — will be described here and respected.

This website

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no third-party scripts, fonts or tracking pixels, and has no contact form. If you email us, we receive your address and whatever you write; we keep it only as long as needed to answer, and we never add you to a mailing list.

Purchases

Optional credit purchases are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's terms and privacy statement. The publisher does not receive your name, address or payment details. Refunds are handled by Microsoft Store support.

No subscription is sold in this version. Installing or using the app creates no recurring charge. Pro is priced in the app, and the plans window states plainly that billing is not enabled yet.

API keys

Keys you enter are used only to authenticate requests to the provider you chose. They are stored locally, are not written to source files and are not uploaded anywhere else. Avoid sharing logs or screenshots that contain keys.

Legal bases and your rights

Because the developer operates no server that stores your data, there is normally nothing for us to export, correct or erase. Where you have contacted us, we process your message on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering support requests, and we delete it when the matter is closed unless a record is needed for accounting or legal reasons.

Where the General Data Protection Regulation or similar law applies, you may ask what personal data we hold about you, ask for a copy, correction or deletion, object to processing, or complain to your local supervisory authority. Write to the address below; we answer within 30 days. Information held by a database, a publisher or an AI provider you chose to use is controlled by that provider — contact them directly, and note that the app tells you which provider you configured.

Retention

Local files remain until you delete them. Support email is kept until the request is resolved. Nothing else is retained, because nothing else is collected.

Security

The app communicates with external services over HTTPS. Local data relies on the security of your Windows account and disk; it is not separately encrypted. Keep Windows updates applied and do not install builds from sources other than the Microsoft Store.

Children

BiblioHarvest is intended for students, researchers and professionals. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and it has no feature that asks for a name, age or contact detail.

Your choices

  • Delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\BiblioHarvest to remove all local data.
  • Leave AI providers unset to keep all processing local.
  • Dismiss the sponsored line; it stays closed for the session.
  • Use the app fully offline for screening and exporting records you already collected.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above is updated and the new version is published at this address before the corresponding app update is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support.biblioharvest@gmail.com.
